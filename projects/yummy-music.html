Yummy Music is an independent label in Tatarstan. I joined in late 2025 as assistant to the head, working on the creative producing side.

Label work is where the artist-facing and the audience-facing parts of this job meet: what a release should be, who it is for, and what has to be built around it so it lands.

Working here is also the most direct answer to the question Музон asks from the outside — what a regional scene needs in order to keep going.
