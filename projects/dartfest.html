Dartfest 2025: 40,000+ attendees, 120 performers. I worked as assistant to the producer of the main stage.

A main stage at that size runs on changeovers. Somebody has to hold the running order, keep artists and their teams moving through it, and absorb the small emergencies before they reach the audience — that was the job.

It is the clearest lesson I have had in scale: the same production thinking as a club night, with no room at all for improvisation.

40,000 people, and the job is the changeover.
