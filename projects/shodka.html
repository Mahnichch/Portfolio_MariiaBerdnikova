SHODKA

  • Kazan
  • Co-organizer
  • 2024 —
  • Music community / concerts

Shodka is a music community in Kazan. We put on concerts — which in practice means doing every unglamorous thing that has to happen before anyone hears a note.

My side of it is production: negotiating with artists, putting together and checking tech riders, running soundchecks, and keeping the schedule honest on the day itself.

It is the part of music work that never appears in the photos and decides everything about how the night goes.

The show is decided at soundcheck.
