MOOZEON

  • Founder
  • 2025 —
  • Platform / media

MoozeOn documents regional and indigenous music scenes across post-Soviet space, starting with Tatarstan — the place I actually know.

These scenes are usually written about from the outside: a short paragraph, a folk instrument, a past tense. The platform exists to do the opposite. Every scene is documented from inside it, by the people making the music, with their disagreements left in.

The premise is that a culture is a living, changing organism rather than a frozen stereotype — so the material is built to keep changing too: recorded voices, local debates, connections out to artists and scenes beyond the region. A working prototype exists and is being filled with real material.
